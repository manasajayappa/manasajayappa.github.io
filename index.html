About

A technical foundation, sharpened into product judgment

I started in software QA, which is where I learned to ask the question that still drives my product work: what actually happens to the user when this ships? Testing interactive web and mobile applications taught me to read a release the way a customer would — not as a set of tickets, but as an experience that either works or doesn't.

That grew into program delivery. At Apple, I coordinated engineering, product, QA, and operations stakeholders through Apple Store App release cycles — managing plans, dependencies, risks, and the status communication that keeps a cross-functional team honest. At Morgan Stanley / E*TRADE, I translated business needs into requirements and user stories for the trading platform, supported sprint planning and backlog discussions, and tracked the reliability risks that decide whether a financial release is safe to ship.

Alongside that, I founded ECLAT, a self-funded pre-launch apparel brand — which has been an unglamorous but excellent education in owning a product end to end: specs, sourcing, MOQs, unit economics, and the tradeoffs you make when the budget is genuinely finite.

I'm now pursuing an MBA at Santa Clara University's Leavey School of Business, with Product School's Product Management and AI Product certifications behind me. I'm looking for Product Management, Product/Program Management, and AI product roles where delivery discipline and customer empathy both matter.
